INFORMED CONSENT FORM: PHASE III CLINICAL TRIAL

Part I: Information Sheet
Introduction:
You are being invited to take part in a research study entitled "Advanced Efficacy of Treatment X on Chronic Respiratory Conditions". Before you decide, you must understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please take the time to read the following information carefully and discuss it with friends, relatives, and your personal physician if you wish.

Purpose of the Research:
The purpose of this study is to test a new investigational drug to determine its safety and efficacy compared to the current standard of care. This drug has been tested in smaller groups, but we now need to evaluate its impact on a larger population to identify rare side effects and long-term outcomes.

Type of Intervention:
The intervention involves the administration of one oral tablet daily for a period of 24 weeks. During this time, you will be required to attend bi-weekly follow-up visits at the clinical site for blood draws, pulmonary function tests, and physical examinations.

Participant Selection:
We are inviting adults between the ages of 18 and 65 who have been diagnosed with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and who are not currently pregnant or breastfeeding.

Voluntary Participation:
Your participation in this research is entirely voluntary. It is your choice whether to participate or not. If you choose not to participate, all the services you receive at this clinic will continue and nothing will change. You may change your mind later and stop participating even if you agreed earlier; this is called withdrawal of consent.

Risks and Discomforts:
By participating in this study, you may experience certain side effects. Common risks include dizziness, nausea, and localized irritation at the site of blood draws. There is also a small risk of more severe allergic reactions. The study team will monitor your health closely to minimize these risks.

Confidentiality:
We will not be sharing information about you to anyone outside of the research team. The information that we collect from this research project will be kept confidential. Information about you that will be collected during the study will be put away and no-one but the researchers will be able to see it. Any information about you will have a number on it instead of your name. Only the researchers will know what your number is and we will lock that information up with a lock and key.

Right to Refuse or Withdraw:
You do not have to take part in this research if you do not wish to do so. You may stop participating at any time without losing any of your rights as a patient. Your treatment at this clinic will not be affected in any way.

Contact Details:
If you have any questions, you can ask them now or later. If you wish to ask questions later, you may contact the Principal Investigator, Dr. [Name], at [Phone Number/Email].
